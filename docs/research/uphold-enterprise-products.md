# Uphold enterprise products — first-party research

> **Scope:** Public product facts only. This note does not establish or imply any individual employee's private responsibilities.

## Verified products

### Uphold Ascent

**What it is / users.** Uphold’s institutional site calls Ascent its platform for institutional investors. It connects to 30+ CeFi, DeFi and Layer-2 venues, smart-routes orders, and is presented for competitive pricing and executing/liquidating large positions with minimal market impact. Safe wording: _institutional digital-asset trading and execution platform._

**Source:** [_Uphold Institutional - Providing an edge to institutional investors_](https://uphold.com/en-us/institutional) (page title).

### Uphold Enterprise API Suite

**What it is / users.** Developer documentation describes APIs for businesses building “enterprise-grade crypto products,” including user onboarding and moving value across banks, cards and crypto. It offers direct backend API access, a drop-in widget, and FIX protocol for real-time quotes/order execution; the FIX material names banks, brokers and high-volume trading as users. Safe wording: _enterprise integration platform for embedded crypto, payments and trading flows._

**Source:** [_Get started with the Uphold Enterprise API Suite - Uphold_](https://developer.uphold.com/get-started/overview) (page title).

### Enterprise/Partner Portal

**Verified fact.** The Enterprise API overview instructs organizations to register at the **Enterprise Portal** and identifies it as the place to enter company and administrator details. I could not verify a distinct Uphold product formally named **“Partner Portal.”** Do not describe it as a separate product without a first-party source; use _Enterprise Portal_ for organization registration/configuration.

**Source:** [_Get started with the Uphold Enterprise API Suite - Uphold_](https://developer.uphold.com/get-started/overview) (page title).

### Topper by Uphold

**What it is / users.** Topper describes itself as an Uphold-developed on/off-ramp for users and Web3 projects: users can buy, sell and send crypto directly to self-custodial wallets. Its business offering is therefore an integration layer for wallet and Web3-product operators, distinct from the broader Uphold consumer wallet/platform.

**Phantom relationship.** Topper’s 2024 official recap lists **Phantom** among its “On-ramp Integrations.” This supports the limited claim that Phantom integrated Topper as a fiat-to-crypto on-ramp; it does **not** establish that Topper is Phantom’s only provider or that Uphold controls Phantom.

**Sources:**

- [_Topper - What’s the Difference Between Topper and Uphold?_](https://www.topperpay.com/blog/the-difference-between-topper-and-uphold) (page title).
- [_Topper - Topper’s Year in Crypto: 2024_](https://www.topperpay.com/blog/toppers-year-in-crypto-2024) (page title).

## How the public offering fits together

Ascent serves institutional execution; the Enterprise API Suite supplies programmatic/embedded product infrastructure; the Enterprise Portal is the documented organization-registration surface; and Topper is the wallet/Web3-facing on/off-ramp. These are public product-positioning statements, not evidence of any particular person’s duties.
